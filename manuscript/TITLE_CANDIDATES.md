state-of-the-art or breakthrough claim; no novelty assertion, because the
manuscript does not establish novelty as a contribution; the causal

## Recommended

> **T1 — Causal Multi-Horizon PM2.5 Forecasting Across a Twelve-Station Urban
> Network: Improvement over Persistence on Average, an Unresolved Severe Tail**

### Rationale

T1 is the only candidate that discharges every constraint at once.

- **It carries both halves of the headline.** The study's defensible result has
  two inseparable parts: engineered causal history beat persistence on average
  across 1–24 h, and persistence remained the strongest severe benchmark.
  Reporting either alone misrepresents the study. T1 puts both in the title, so
  the limitation survives even the shortest possible reading. T2 also does this
  but reads as a slogan rather than a description.
- **"Causal" is load-bearing, not decorative.** It names the constraint that
  makes the evaluation meaningful: no input postdates its forecast origin, and
  future observed meteorology is prohibited by construction.
- **"Twelve-Station Urban Network" states the scope without overclaiming it.**
  It signals multi-site evidence while making the single-city limitation plain,
  which a reviewer should not have to reach the Limitations section to discover.
- **It avoids the spatial trap.** T6 foregrounds cross-station context, which
  invites a propagation reading the Phase-7 diagnostics do not support
  (Spearman(distance, attention) = +0.136289). T1 leaves it out of the title.
- **"Unresolved" is the accurate word.** Not "failure", not "collapse". The
  severe tail is an open problem, and the study measured how open it is.

T3 and T4 are the strongest alternates if a venue objects to a two-clause
title; both keep the sealed-test framing but drop the severe-tail result from
the title, which then has to be carried entirely by the abstract.

T5 is the most quotable and the most rhetorically risky: "Average Accuracy Is
Not Tail Accuracy" is a general claim the study supports for this dataset and
these models only.
